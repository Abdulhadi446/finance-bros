# Compound Ledger Usage Guide

Compound Ledger is a browser-only scenario calculator. It is designed for quick comparisons, not for predicting market results.

## A one-month estimate

1. Select `Months` under `Calculate by`.
2. Set the horizon to `1` or another number of months.
3. Enter the starting balance and monthly contribution.
4. Review the ending value and the monthly dividend row.

## A long-term scenario

1. Select an asset preset.
2. Keep `Years` selected and choose the time horizon.
3. Set an annual contribution raise if needed.
4. Choose whether dividends are paid as cash or reinvested.
5. Compare the ending value, total invested, gain, and real purchasing power.

## A portfolio blend

Select `Portfolio blend`, set the four contribution shares, and make sure the allocation reaches 100%. The combined result uses each preset's own growth, fee, yield, dividend-growth, and tax assumptions.

## Stress and retirement tests

`±25% range` adds illustrative low and high paths around the smooth line. It is not a Monte Carlo simulation. To explore withdrawals, enter a monthly amount and choose the starting year. Withdrawals begin at that year and cannot reduce a scenario balance below zero.

## Reading dividends

The Dividend income panel uses the cash-dividend scenario. It shows gross dividends, after-tax dividends, an average monthly amount, and the latest annual after-tax dividend as a yield on total invested capital. The table provides annual rows in Years mode and monthly rows in Months mode.
