# Compound Ledger FAQ

## Is this investment advice?

No. It is a simplified planning tool. Presets and user-entered rates are assumptions, not guarantees or recommendations.

## Why does the app use monthly growth?

Contributions, fees, dividends, and withdrawals are modeled month by month. Annual rates are converted into monthly inputs where appropriate.

## Does the volatility toggle simulate the market?

No. It draws two deterministic stress paths using 75% and 125% of the average monthly growth. It does not model random returns, correlations, drawdowns, or sequence-of-returns risk.

## Are dividend taxes applied to reinvested dividends?

Yes. The reinvested scenario adds after-tax dividends to the balance. The cash scenario keeps after-tax dividends outside the invested balance and includes them in the cash total.

## What is not modeled?

Capital-gains tax on sale, brokerage costs, currency movement, asset correlation, changing tax rules, dividend cuts, inflation uncertainty, and security-specific performance are outside the model.

## Where are my inputs stored?

Inputs are stored in this browser's local storage only. Clear the site's local storage to reset them, or click a preset to replace the current assumptions.
