What's modeled: monthly contributions (increased once a year if you set a raise), constant monthly price growth, a dividend yield paid monthly and optionally reinvested, dividend tax, an annual fee drag, and inflation to translate the result into today's purchasing power.

What isn't: real markets don't move in a straight line — an average of 1%/month is not the same as gaining exactly 1% every single month. Capital gains tax on selling, transaction costs, and dividend cuts aren't modeled either. This is a planning sketch, not a forecast or investment advice.
